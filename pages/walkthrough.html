[ 01. PHASE PROGRESSION MANUAL ]

Follow the sequential directives below to breach the viral quarantine perimeter. Monitored hostile biological units are active in Sub-Level B2.

  1. Infiltrate the Central Decontamination Airlock Move silently along the northern pipeline. Use sprint Shift + W to cross exposed steam vents during pressure vent cycles. Crouch using Ctrl to remain undetected beneath acoustic sensors.
  2. Acquire Sector B Security Card & Firearm Upgrade Locate the security locker behind the ruptured glass partition. Interact using E to obtain the High-Caliber Compensator. Press Tab to open inventory and combine weapon parts.
  3. Engage Emergency Power Sub-Turbines Navigate the flooded lower corridor. Hold Shift to wade rapidly through toxic cooling runoffs. Ready sidearm with Right Click and discharge with Left Click when mutated crimson heads breach the mesh gate.
  4. Synthesize Antigen Compound-Alpha Access the biometric mixing centrifuge in Lab B4. Rotate centrifuge cylinders using Q and E until pressure stabilizes at 42.8 PSI. Press Space to lock synthesis.
  5. Subdue Nemesis Prototype Incursion When the heavy blast doors buckle, execute a combat dodge using Space + S to avoid the overhead tentacle swing. Lure the entity beneath overhead cryogenic pipes and shoot release valves.
